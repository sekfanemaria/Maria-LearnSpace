My name is Maria Sekfane, and I am a Computer Science student specializing in Software Engineering. My interest in web development started during my third year of university, when I worked on my first project: a web application called IPYLIA.

Today, I am learning and practicing several programming languages and web technologies to become a skilled and versatile web developer. I continuously explore new technologies, work on personal projects, and challenge myself to improve my technical skills.

My goal is to keep learning, gain valuable experience, and create modern, useful, and user-friendly web applications.
